Crowdshipping participation choices
Transportation and Urban Studio E
Dataset description | Prepared 10 November 2026

Context
-------
This case study examines whether people will deliver parcels as occasional carriers, either during an existing trip or by starting a new trip from home. The data combine stated choices with personal, mobility, shopping and attitudinal information. They support analysis of delivery participation, compensation and travel-time trade-offs. [1, 2]

Data Collection
---------------
The online survey was conducted in the Netherlands in July 2022. Recruitment used personal contacts, social media and public flyers; eligibility required residence in the Netherlands and age above 18. Of 298 responses, 250 were completed. The design used two blocks with six tasks per context; public-transport commuters were only offered the home-based experiment. [2]

The local file database_final.csv is semicolon-delimited, with 2,400 choice-situation rows, 61 columns and 250 distinct ID values. Each row records one choice and contains the attributes of the available alternatives. Respondents contribute between 1 and 12 rows; this is an unbalanced panel.

Choice experiments and alternatives
-----------------------------------
Context / local condition | Alternatives | Rows
Commute-based: AV_Trips = 1 | Deliver before the activity; deliver after the activity; do not deliver. | 980
Home-based: AV_Home = 1, AV_PickUp = 1 | Deliver by bicycle (generic delivery label); stay home. | 1,060
Home-based: AV_Home = 1, AV_Car = AV_Bike = 1 | Deliver by car; deliver by bicycle; stay home. | 360

The generic home-based delivery response denotes bicycle delivery in the bicycle-only scenario, as clarified by the paper and the local availability pattern. Together, the two bicycle labels account for 534 choices. [2]

Observation identifiers
-----------------------
Use (ID, AV_Trips, ChoiceTask) as the local choice-situation key: all 2,400 combinations are unique. ChoiceTask restarts across contexts, so (ID, ChoiceTask) alone is insufficient. Retain ID for panel estimation or respondent-level train/test splits.



Variables: identifiers, availability and alternatives
-----------------------------------------------------
Names below match the CSV exactly. The notation j = 1, 2 covers both Alt1_ and Alt2_ columns. Time is in minutes and monetary attributes are in euros. Structural zeros and the literal text "Not NA" occur in fields outside their applicable context. [1, 2]

Name | Description
ID | Respondent identifier.
ChoiceTask | Choice-task number, 1-6 within each context.
value | Selected alternative, stored as a text label; see the choice-frequency table.
Block | Experimental design block, 1 or 2.
AV_Trips / AV_Home | Context indicators: 1 for a commute-based / home-based row, respectively; 0 otherwise.
AV_Car / AV_Bike | Both equal 1 in home-based rows offering separate car and bicycle options; both are 0 elsewhere. Not general vehicle-ownership indicators.
AV_PickUp | Equals 1 for the home-based bicycle-only delivery option; 0 elsewhere.
Base_TT | Reference travel time for the existing trip; 0 outside commute-based rows.
Car | Commute scenario mode: Bike or Car; Not NA outside commute-based rows.
Base_Cost_Car | Reference car cost for the existing trip; only substantively relevant to car scenarios.
Alt1_TOD / Alt2_TOD | Before activity / After activity. These identify the two commute delivery alternatives.
Altj_parcels | Number of parcels for commute alternative j: 1, 2 or 3.
Altj_TT | Total travel time for commute alternative j. If modeling extra travel time, derive Altj_TT - Base_TT.
Altj_PUP | Parcel-locker indicator for commute alternative j: Yes / No.
Altj_Cost_Car | Car travel cost for commute alternative j; apply only to car scenarios.
Altj_Rem | Total remuneration for commute alternative j.
Bike_TT | Travel time for home-based bicycle delivery, including the generic bicycle-only option.
PUP | Parcel-locker indicator for home-based delivery: Yes / No.
Rem | Remuneration for home-based delivery.
Car_TT / Car_Cost | Travel time / cost for the home-based car alternative; structurally 0 where it is unavailable.

Preparation notes
-----------------
Keep the original strings when importing the file. The separator is a semicolon, while some category values contain commas. "Not NA" and "NA" are literal source values; interpret them by column and experimental context.

Reconstruct availability from the context flags before forming a common alternative set. In particular, AV_Bike = 0 does not mean bicycle delivery is unavailable when AV_PickUp = 1.

Treat 999 as an out-of-scale code pending clarification; exclude or mark it missing in analyses of 1-5 attitudes. Do not infer the meaning or direction of an individual attitude item from its column number.
