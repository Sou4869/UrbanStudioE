Delivery option choice data
Transportation and Urban Studio E
Survey conducted in 2021 by Oyama at SIT with Imura and Nishinari at UTokyo and Yamato Holdings

Choice variable:
    - 1000: Next delivery, 
    - 2000: Normal delivery,
    - >= 3000: Scheduled (Specified) delivery
        - 3021: Scheduled delivery for "2" days after the order date & the "1"st (earliest) time slot
        - 3022: Scheduled delivery for "2" days after the order date & the "2"nd (other) time slot
        - 3023: Scheduled delivery for "2" days after the order date & the "3"rd (latest) time slot
        - 3031: Scheduled delivery for "3" days after the order date & the "1"st (earliest) time slot
        - ...
        - 3083: Scheduled delivery for "8" days after the order date & the "3"rd (latest) time slot
