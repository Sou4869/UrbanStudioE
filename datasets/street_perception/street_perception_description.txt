Street-based neighborhood perception data
Transportation and Urban Studio E
Survey conducted in 2020 by Oyama lab at SIT

Binary choice variable = "included":
    indicates if an individual n perceives street segment i as being included in the neighborhood
   
Attributes
- distance: from the representative point to the street
- tree, building, road, sky, other: pixel values of the street image (0~100)
- inner_river, area, and avenue: if the street is inside of boundaries of river, administrative district, and avenue
- cos_simil, euclid_dist: dissimilarity measure of streetscape compared to the average scape of the neighborhood

More info:
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cities.2024.104898
